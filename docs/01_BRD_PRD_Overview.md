# Business & Product Requirements Document (BRD & PRD)
**Project Name:** Unified Product OS (Standalone Discovery & Delivery Platform)
**Primary Objective:** Build a 100% standalone SaaS platform that replicates and unifies the capabilities of Jira Product Discovery (Strategy) and Jira Software (Agile Execution) into a single, seamless codebase. No third-party Jira APIs will be used.

## 1. Core Value Proposition
* **Native Traceability:** 100% visibility from executive-level strategic initiatives (Ideas) down to engineering story points and tasks, with zero synchronization lag.
* **Unified Governance:** A single source of truth for the Project Management Office (PMO) to manage resources, timelines, and cross-functional priorities.
* **Frictionless UI:** A clean, lightning-fast interface free of legacy enterprise bloat, adhering to minimalist corporate design standards (Apple / BCG / Google Cloud UI).

## 2. Product Modules

### Module A: Product Discovery (Strategy Phase)
* **Idea Backlog & Triage:** A dynamic, spreadsheet-like table to capture product ideas. Must support custom columns such as Impact Score, Effort, Target Quarter, and RICE scoring.
* **Strategic Roadmap:** Visual timeline and Kanban board grouped by strategic business initiatives (e.g., Now / Next / Later).
* **Executive Roll-up:** Automated calculation of an Idea's progress percentage based on the completion status of its underlying delivery tasks.

### Module B: Agile Delivery (Execution Phase)
* **Epic & Backlog Management:** Creation and management of Epics, User Stories, Tasks, and Bugs. Must support agile estimation using Story Points.
* **Sprint Planning & Active Sprints:** Drag-and-drop Kanban board (To Do, In Progress, Review, Done) tied to strict Sprint timeboxes.
* **Velocity & Burn-down:** Real-time metric dashboards to track engineering team performance across sprints.
